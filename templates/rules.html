1League Objective

The Kohi Dojo Cantaball League Season 1 exists to:

  • Build community among TechRise students.
  • Celebrate Aba's traditional Cantaball culture.
  • Create a fun and competitive environment.
  • Promote teamwork and sportsmanship.
  • Support Kohi Dojo events and student engagement.

2Eligibility

  • Registration is open to all TechRise students.
  • Each team must register before the weekly deadline.
  • Maximum of 20 teams per week.
  • Teams that do not qualify for the weekly finals may register again in future weeks (subject to available slots).

3Team Composition

  • Maximum 2 players per team
  • Teams may choose:
    • Team Name
    • Team Captain
    • Team Colours
  • Players cannot represent more than one team in the same weekly season.

4Registration

Registration Fee:

  • ₦3000 per team

Registration includes:

  • Entry into the weekly league.
  • Official fixture schedule.
  • Player profile feature on Kohi Dojo social media.
  • Access to League Awards.
  • Eligibility for Champions League qualification.
  • Season participation certificate (digital).

5League Format

Groups:

  • Group A: Teams 1–10
  • Group B: Teams 11–20

Each group contains 10 teams. Every team plays every other team in its group once.

Each team therefore plays: 9 league matches

6Point System

  • Win — 3 Points
  • Draw — 1 Point
  • Loss — 0 Points

7League Table Tiebreakers

If two or more teams finish on equal points:

  1. Goal Difference
  2. Goals Scored
  3. Head-to-Head Result
  4. Playoff Match (if required)

8Match Format

Each match lasts:

20 minutes
No halftime.
The referee's decision is final.

9Matchday Schedule

Monday – Friday

Opening Ceremony - 5:30 PM

First Match - 6:00 PM

Final Match - 10:05 PM

Saturday

Opening - 12:00 PM

League Matches - 12:30 PM

Semi Finals - Afternoon

Final & Awards - Evening

10Knockout Stage

Top Two Teams from each Group qualify.

Semi Final 1: 1st Group A vs 2nd Group B
Semi Final 2: 1st Group B vs 2nd Group A
Grand Final: Winner of SF1 vs Winner of SF2

11Champions League & Grand Finale Awards

The Kohi Dojo Cantaball League is played across four qualifying weeks, culminating in Champions League Week, where the best teams compete for the ultimate title during Flow! 04 – Demo Day.

  • The top four teams from each qualifying week advance to the Champions League.
  • Only qualified teams are eligible to compete for the Season 1 championship.

14Fair Play

Players must:

  • Respect officials.
  • Respect opponents.
  • Respect spectators.
  • Respect the venue.
  • Respect Kohi Dojo staff.

Unsportsmanlike behaviour may lead to:

  • Match warning
  • Point deduction
  • Match forfeiture
  • Suspension
  • Disqualification

15Equipment

  • Only official league Cantaball equipment may be used.
  • No modifications are permitted during matches.

16Match Officials

Every match will have:

  • Referee
  • Timekeeper
  • Scorekeeper
  • Commentator
  • Media Team
Their decisions regarding match administration are final.

17Media

By registering, teams agree that:

  • Photos may be taken.
  • Videos may be recorded.
  • Interviews may be conducted.
  • Match highlights may be shared on Kohi Dojo and LearnFactory platforms.

18Protests

If a team wishes to protest:

  • Notify the referee immediately after the match.
  • Submit the protest before the end of the day's fixtures.
  • League officials will review the matter before the next matchday.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

When and where are matches held?

Matches are held every Saturday at 2:00 PM at our indoor facility in Lagos. The exact address is provided upon registration.

How much is the registration fee?

The weekly entry fee is N5,000 per team of 2 players. This covers the court rental, equipment, and prize pool contribution.

What happens if my team can't make it?

Please notify us 24 hours in advance if your team cannot attend. Unfortunately, we cannot offer refunds for no-shows as spots are limited.

Can I change my team partner?

Yes, you can change partners between weeks, but not during a week's tournament. Both players on your registered team must be present.

How are points calculated?

Winners receive 2 points, losers receive 0. In case of ties in the standings, we use point difference (points scored minus points conceded) as a tie-breaker.
