About the League

What is the CANTABALL League?

The Kohi Dojo CANTABALL League is a weekly indoor sports competition held every Saturday at our premium facility in Lagos. Teams of two compete in fast-paced, skill-based matches that test precision, strategy, and teamwork.

Founded in 2023, the league has grown from a small gathering of enthusiasts to one of Lagos' most exciting weekly sporting events. With professional refereeing, live score tracking, and prize money for top performers, we bring the thrill of professional competition to amateur athletes.

  • Weekly Saturday matches
  • Professional referees and scoring
  • Cash prizes for top 4 teams
  • Individual player statistics
  • Live match updates
