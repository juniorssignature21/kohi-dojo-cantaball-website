About the League

What is the Kohi Dojo Cantaball League?

The Kohi Dojo Cantaball League is a weekly indoor tabletop football competition hosted at the LearnFactory TechRise Campus by Kohi Dojo × NueLuks Studio. Inspired by the iconic street game played across Aba with bottle corks and paper goalposts, the league reimagines Cantaball into a professionally organized tournament that celebrates competition, creativity and community.

Every week, 20 teams of two players battle through a round-robin league, with the top teams earning qualification for the Champions League Finals, which will be held during Flow! 04 – Demo Day. Beyond the competition, the league serves as a community hub where students connect, enjoy great food from Kohi Dojo, and create unforgettable moments together.

Whether you're playing for the trophy, the prize money or simply for the love of the game, the Kohi Dojo Cantaball League is where Aba's tradition meets a new generation of innovation.

  • 20 Teams Competing Every Week

  • Cash Prizes & Kohi Dojo Rewards
  • Team Features Across Social Media

  • Food, Coffee & Community at Kohi Dojo
  • Grand Championship During Flow! 04
