We Are Kohi Dojo

Kohi Dojo is the creative café of NueLuks Studio — an Afro-Japanese inspired café and creative community where coffee, storytelling, music, art and culture come together. We're a space for connection, creativity and meaningful conversations, celebrating the fusion of African and Japanese aesthetics through thoughtfully crafted coffee experiences, curated events, artistic expression and cultural exchange.

Whether you're here to work, create, perform, collaborate or simply unwind, our café is a home for dreamers, makers and storytellers.
